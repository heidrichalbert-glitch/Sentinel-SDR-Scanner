# Sentinel-SDR-Scanner
Sentinel SDR Scanner - Anti-Surveillance Tool

<img width="669" height="158" alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/35435876-b6cb-4537-a0ed-dd84d7560150" />

Requirements:
- Windows 10 or 11 required (the .py file in the installation folder also runs on Linux)
- At least 4 GB of RAM
- Wi-Fi network adapter
- GPS support
- Bluetooth (4.0 or 5.0)

Hardware:
- Support for 1–3 SDRs (software-defined radio, available starting at $20)
  Minimum of 1 SDR (e.g., NOOLEC NESDR); HackRF recommended for best results

Main Features:
- Scans all typical radio frequencies and detects hidden
  bugs, microphones, and surveillance cameras with 90–95% accuracy
- Triangulates and calculates distances to detected signals, indicating direction,
  signal strength, and approximate distance; includes an audible alert for assistance
- Detects and lists all Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth connections, and other radio
  transmitters in the vicinity; frequency ranges are freely configurable
- Powerful jammer; detected signals can be directly jammed/blocked
  to disable unwanted surveillance
- All devices can be logged; this makes it possible to expose surveillance operations
  and potential pursuers on the go

Unique in its form and completely free to use! Fight against governmental surveillance!
